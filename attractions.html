Rome, Italy

After-Hours Colosseum & Forum Walk

Skip the crowds entirely. A private archaeologist leads you beneath the arena floor and through the Roman Forum at twilight — the way emperors once saw it.

3 hours ★ 4.9 (412)
Nature
Santorini, Greece

Caldera Sunset Sail on a Wooden Schooner

Glide past the volcanic cliffs of Oia aboard a restored 1950s schooner. Anchor at Red Beach, swim the hot springs, and dine on the deck as the sun melts.

5 hours ★ 4.95 (287)
Culinary
Kyoto, Japan

Private Tea Ceremony with a Geiko

In a 200-year-old machiya in Gion, a senior geiko hosts an intimate chanoyu followed by a kaiseki dinner. Conversation, koto music, and rare matcha included.

2.5 hours ★ 5.0 (96)
Cultural
Marrakech, Morocco

Hidden Riads & Souk Artisans Walk

A novelist-historian threads you through the medina's quietest alleys, into the workshops of master tile-cutters, dyers, and a fourth-generation rug-maker.

4 hours ★ 4.88 (203)
Nature
Vatnajökull, Iceland

Ice Cave Helicopter Expedition

Take off from Reykjavík and land on Europe's largest ice cap. A glaciologist guides you into a sapphire-blue ice cave only accessible three weeks each winter.

Full day ★ 4.97 (58)
Wellness
Ubud, Bali

Sunrise Yoga & Healer's Blessing

Begin in a riverside jungle pavilion at first light, then receive a traditional Balian water blessing at a 1,000-year-old temple known only to villagers.

3 hours ★ 4.92 (174)

Experience of the Month

A Night at the Alhambra, After Closing.

Where
Granada
Duration
4 hrs
Group Size
Up to 6
From
$1,240

After the last visitor leaves at sunset, the gates of Spain's most-visited monument close behind you. A private historian, a flamenco guitarist, and a Moorish-cuisine chef accompany you through the Nasrid Palaces and Generalife gardens — by candlelight.
