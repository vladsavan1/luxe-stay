Our Story

Who We Are

Born from a Passion for Exceptional Hospitality

Founded in 2018 by a team of seasoned travellers and hospitality experts, LuxeStay began with a simple belief: that every stay should be an extraordinary experience, not just a transaction.

We've spent years building relationships with the world's most remarkable hoteliers, curating a collection that spans boutique urban escapes, remote wilderness lodges, and legendary palace hotels.

What Drives Us

Our Values

Meticulous Curation

Every property is personally inspected against 120 quality criteria. Less than 8% of applicant hotels make our platform.

Genuine Relationships

We're not a booking engine — we're a trusted partner. Our hotel relationships give you access to upgrades and experiences unavailable elsewhere.

Responsible Travel

We actively promote eco-certified properties and partner with hotels that invest in local communities and environmental stewardship.

Effortless Experience

From first search to final check-out, every touchpoint is designed to feel intuitive, seamless, and enjoyable.

Personalised Service

Our human concierge team is available around the clock to craft bespoke itineraries and arrange special requests.

Best Price Promise

Find a lower price elsewhere for the same room and we'll match it. Always get the best value for your investment.
